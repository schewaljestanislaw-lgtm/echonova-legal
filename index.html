EchoNova Player — Support

EchoNova Player is a music player for audio files stored on your iPhone. Your music and playlists stay on your device; no account is needed.

Contact

Questions, bug reports and feedback: echonova.player@icloud.com

Frequently asked

How do I add music? Tap + and choose MP3, WAV or OGG files in the Files picker.

Will my songs move to a new iPhone? No. Songs and playlists are stored only on this device.

Частые вопросы

Как добавить музыку? Нажмите + и выберите файлы MP3, WAV или OGG в «Файлах».

Перенесутся ли песни на новый iPhone? Нет. Песни и плейлисты хранятся только на этом устройстве.
